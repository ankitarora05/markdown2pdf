Privacy Policy

This policy explains what markdown2pdf collects, why it is used, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you.

Effective: 15 October 2026Last updated: 15 October 2026Version: 1.0
In short: Account details are stored so you can sign in and recover your account. Markdown is sent to our server to create a preview or PDF, but the app does not save your documents as account files. Your browser stores your draft locally. We do not currently run advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Who is responsible

For this policy, “markdown2pdf”, “we”, and “us” refer to the service created by Ankit Arora. Where applicable privacy law uses the term controller or business, the service provider is responsible for the personal information described here. You can contact Ankit through the creator profile. This online service may be used from different countries; local notices and rights can vary.

2. Information we process

Account and authentication information

When you register, we collect your username, email address, first and last name, a password verifier (a one-way Argon2 hash, not your readable password), account creation time, and a record of the Terms of Service acceptance and policy versions. We do not require email verification at signup. Password reset requests create a short-lived, single-use reset token; only its hash is stored. Session refresh-token hashes, expiry times, revocation status, and session-family identifiers are stored to keep accounts signed in securely and detect token reuse.

Documents and conversion requests

Text you enter is saved in your browser's local storage as a draft. When you ask for a preview or PDF, the relevant Markdown is transmitted to our application server and processed to return the result. The application does not intentionally store Markdown or generated PDFs in your account or database. Internet and hosting providers may process limited technical request information in their infrastructure logs; avoid submitting secrets or sensitive personal information.

Preferences and cookie choices

Your selected theme and draft are stored in browser local storage. A strictly necessary, HttpOnly refresh-session cookie is used when you sign in. If you set a cookie preference, we store the selected categories and policy version in this browser and may record the preference event without attaching your name or email. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

Support and technical information

If you contact the creator, the information you choose to include is processed to respond. The hosting or infrastructure provider may also process ordinary technical information such as request time, IP address, browser headers, and security events under its own service arrangements. The application does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers.

6. Retention and security

We retain account information while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward when needed for security, dispute resolution, legal obligations, and account recovery. Refresh-session records expire or are revoked; password-reset records are short-lived and single-use. Policy and cookie preference records may be retained as needed to evidence the choice and meet legal obligations. Browser drafts remain until you clear them or clear browser storage. Transient conversion inputs are processed to respond to your request and are not intended to be retained as documents.

We use measures intended to protect information, including one-way password hashing, limited session token storage, HTTPS in production, and access controls. No online transmission or storage method is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a unique password and notify us if you believe your account has been compromised.

Some rights have exceptions, and we may need to verify your request. You may also request account deletion by contacting the creator. California and certain other US state residents may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of covered personal information and to opt out of certain sales, sharing, or targeted advertising. You can change optional cookie settings using Cookie preferences in the converter or the footer control. You may withdraw optional-cookie consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
