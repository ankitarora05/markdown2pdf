Cookie Policy

Here is how markdown2pdf uses cookies and similar browser storage, and how to choose or change optional uses.

Effective: 15 October 2026Last updated: 15 October 2026Version: 1.0
Your choice matters. The current site uses a strictly necessary sign-in cookie and browser local storage for your draft and preferences. No advertising or analytics cookies are active. Choose “Essential only” to continue without optional tracking, or open Cookie preferences from the homepage footer at any time.

1. What this policy covers

“Cookies” includes browser cookies and similar technologies such as local storage. This policy covers their use by markdown2pdf, created by Ankit Arora. The Privacy Policy explains broader information handling. Browser, hosting, and third-party technologies can change; this page will be updated when our use changes.

3. Your cookie choices

On the converter, you are asked to choose before optional technologies are enabled. “Essential only” acknowledges this policy and allows the strictly necessary and functional technologies described above; it does not opt you into advertising or analytics. “Allow optional” records your current preference for the optional analytics and advertising categories. Because no optional trackers are active today, making that choice does not currently activate any tracker and is not blanket consent to unknown vendors, technologies, or purposes. If optional technologies are introduced, we will describe them and request any specific consent required by law before activating them.

You can change or withdraw optional choices at any time using the highlighted Cookie preferences control in the homepage footer. You may also block or clear storage in your browser settings; blocking necessary cookies may prevent account login, session refresh, or PDF export while signed in. The public editor and preview remain available.

In places where consent is required by law, we will obtain the applicable choice before setting or reading non-essential cookies or similar technologies. Necessary technologies are limited to what is needed to provide a feature you request. An acknowledgment of this policy is not treated as consent to optional tracking.

4. Future analytics, advertisements, and affiliate marketing

If we add analytics, advertising, or affiliate tracking, we will update this page with the provider, exact technology, purpose, and lifespan, and make the relevant controls available. Where required, those technologies will remain blocked until you opt in. We will not condition use of the core converter on accepting optional cookies. Affiliate links, if added, will be identified clearly near the link; a merchant may set its own cookies after you choose to visit its site, under its own policy.

You can learn more about account and document information in the Privacy Policy. Any future advertising program may also provide separate ad-preference controls where legally required.

5. Browser controls and “Do Not Track”

Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies and clear local storage. Controls differ by browser and device. Blocking all storage may cause the editor to forget your draft or theme and may sign you out. The Service does not currently respond to “Do Not Track” signals with a separate behavior; optional tracking is not currently active, and any future technologies will follow the choices and notices required by applicable law.

6. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when technologies, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated. If a change requires renewed consent, optional technologies will remain off until you make the required choice.
